Teman Siap

Aplikasi belajar persiapan pernikahan yang ramah, taktil, dan memberdayakan.

🛡️ Playful Guardrails

no hearts or energy limits that block learning; no leaderboards or public comparisons; no streak loss shaming; no countdown pressure or fake scarcity; no variable/random rewards near any money action; no celebration tied to the amount saved or spent (celebrate practice, not balances); mascot never guilt-trips; every sound/animation/haptic can be turned off.

Tombol Taktil (Chunky Buttons)

Bayangan bawah 4px solid yang menyusut ke 0 saat disentuh/ditekan. Sentuh tombol untuk merasakan mekanik fisiknya:

1. Primary Teal (.btn-teal / .primary)
2. Saffron (.btn-saffron / .saffron-btn)
3. Relational Plum (.btn-plum / .plum-btn)
4. Tunas Green (.btn-tunas — Keberhasilan)
5. Soft Coral (.btn-coral — Penekanan Lembut)
6. Sunny Yellow (.btn-sunny — Apresiasi)
7. Secondary Card (.btn-secondary / .secondary)
