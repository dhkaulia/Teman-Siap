# Teman Siap — Project Guidelines & AI Agent Rules

## Teman Siap design rules

TEXT BUDGET (hard rules, mobile first, 360px)
- Screen title: max 4 words. One supporting line: max 10 words, or none.
- Card or row: icon + title (max 4 words) + optional 6-word hint. No paragraphs.
- Anything longer goes behind a "Pelajari" tap (bottom sheet) and is never open by default.
- Teaching content is delivered as interaction (swipe cards, tap-to-match, choose A or B), not as reading. Max 12 words per card face.
- One primary action per screen. Admin or setup controls live in a settings sheet.
- Unbuilt features are not shown (no "Segera Hadir" cards).
- Icons: one SVG set (Lucide or Phosphor, self-hosted). No emoji as UI icons.
Conflicts between a skill's advice and DESIGN.md or these rules: our rules win.

## Anti-Pressure Core Philosophy
- Zero countdown timers, zero artificial urgency, zero guilt framing ("kamu akan kehilangan", "jangan sampai terlewat").
- Benih (seeds) are cosmetic-only, never expire, and are never awarded based on money amounts.
- Weekly gentle ritme with 2 automatic libur protection per month. A neutral reset ("Mulai lagi kapan saja, tamanmu tetap ada") never removes seeds, items, or growth.
- Private milestones shown in Saya as a Kebun grid; no leaderboards, no couple competition, no comparison metrics.
- Emotional and financial safety: "Ini terasa tidak nyaman" persistent exit is always available.
